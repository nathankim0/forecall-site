Forecall

Delete your Forecall account

An account is only created if you sign in (the Sign in button at the top of Settings). You can delete it, together with your backup, in either of these ways.

In the app

  1. Open Forecall, go to Settings and open your account at the top.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The account, the backup and any stored files are deleted from our server right away. Records on your device stay until you erase them or uninstall the app.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email contact@bible-habit.com from the address you signed in with (or tell us the Apple or Google account you used). We delete the account and its backup within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is kept

Nothing from your account or backup. Subscription records that Apple or Google sent us (product, price, country, transaction number) are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires; they do not contain your name or email. Subscriptions themselves are managed and cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account.
