Forecall

A prediction journal for your own life

Forecall is a journal for predictions about your own life. Write down what you think will happen, say how sure you are (50 to 99%), and judge it when the day comes. Over time it shows your hit rate, a 0 to 100 call score and how well your confidence matches what actually happens. There is no money, betting, odds or prizes of any kind.
